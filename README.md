## 🚀 Current Research

### 🧠 Foundation Models for Medical Imaging

Building end-to-end deep learning pipelines for **3D segmentation, anomaly detection,
and multimodal classification** across **10,000+ 3D volumes from 9 public and
clinical datasets**.

I work with models including **DINOv2, BioMedCLIP, MONAI, and MedSAM**, with
multi-GPU distributed training on **SLURM/HPC infrastructure** and reproducible
experiment tracking and evaluation.

### 🧬 Unpaired Generative Learning for Amyloid PET Harmonization

Developing a **Feature-Mapping CycleGAN (FM-CycleGAN)** for harmonizing regional
amyloid PET measurements across PiB and FBP tracers without requiring paired scans
during training.

- Trained on **5,433 unpaired PET scans** across multiple neuroimaging cohorts
- Uses residual MLP generators with adversarial, cycle-consistency, and identity losses
- Improved inter-tracer mcSUVR correlation from **R = 0.922 → R = 0.963**
  on an independent paired test cohort (**p < 0.001**)
- **Manuscript in preparation**

### 🩻 Multimodal AI for Thyroid Nodule Risk Stratification

Developing multimodal models for classifying incidental thyroid nodules using
**CT imaging + radiology-report text**.

- Evaluated **2D, 3D, and multimodal architectures** on a 454-patient clinical cohort
- Fine-tuned **BioMedCLIP vision and text encoders**
- Benchmarked against ResNet, U-Net, and MONAI 3D architectures
- Achieved **0.768 AUC** with multimodal image-text fusion on a 91-patient
  internal holdout cohort
- **Manuscript in preparation**

### ⌚ Foundation Models for Wearable Health

Developing time-series learning pipelines using **minute-level Fitbit heart-rate data
from 1,158 participants** in the **All of Us Research Program**.

Current work explores time-series foundation-model representations for longitudinal
physiological signals and **chronic vs. episodic migraine phenotype classification**.

### 🚗 Foundation Models for Naturalistic Driving

Investigating whether foundation models can identify patterns associated with
**mild cognitive impairment (MCI)** from naturalistic driving behavior.

Current work includes building subject-separated multivariate time-series datasets
from **speed, accelerometer, gyroscope, and vehicle-motion signals** and evaluating
models including **MOMENT, Moirai, and Mantis** for representation learning and
classification.
