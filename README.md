## About Me

I'm a Computer Science Ph.D. researcher at Arizona State University, advised by
**Dr. Teresa Wu** at the **ASU–Mayo Center for Innovative Imaging**.

My research focuses on developing and adapting **foundation models, generative models,
and multimodal learning methods** for healthcare applications. I work across
**3D medical imaging, neuroimaging, physiological time series, and wearable data**,
with an emphasis on precision health, early prediction and detection, computational
oncology, and clinically meaningful AI.

My current work spans large-scale 3D medical imaging pipelines, multimodal
image-text learning, generative PET harmonization, and foundation models for
longitudinal physiological and sensor data.
